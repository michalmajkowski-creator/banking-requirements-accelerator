# Idea Refinement

Capture the problem, affected customers and processes, desired measurable outcomes, stakeholders, constraints, assumptions, non-goals, risks, and source evidence. Separate facts from hypotheses. Ask focused questions for missing decision-critical information. Produce a structured brief; do not invent regulation, policy, or architecture facts. Require Business Owner review.
