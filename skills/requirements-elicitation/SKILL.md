# Requirements Elicitation

Restate the confirmed problem and scope, classify unknowns as blocking or advisory, and ask one coherent group of questions at a time. Cover actors, triggers, happy paths, exceptions, business rules, data, controls, volumes, service levels, dependencies, and non-goals. Record each answer with its source. Return an elicitation brief and open-question log; do not silently fill gaps.
